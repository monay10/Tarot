## Full description (max 4000 characters)

🔮 The cards await you.

Mystic Tarot brings the classic tarot experience to your pocket with all 22 Major Arcana. Pick from the fanned deck, watch your card turn, and read an interpretation crafted for you — fully offline, fully free.

✨ FOUR SPREADS
• Card of the Day — one card bound to the date; it stays yours all day.
• Past • Present • Future — see the flow of your story in three cards.
• Yes / No — hold a question and let the cards whisper the answer.
• Love Spread — You, Them, and the Bond between.

💫 PERSONAL READING ENGINE
Type your question and have it interpreted alongside your cards. The engine detects your topic (love, career, money, health), weighs card combinations and reversals, and writes a flowing, personal reading. Every reading is unique — no internet needed, and your data never leaves your device.

📖 TAROT JOURNAL & STREAK
Every reading is saved automatically. Browse your history, discover your card of the month, and grow your daily streak. 🔥

🌙 CRAFTED WITH CARE
• Original hand-drawn, gold-line illustrations for every card
• Starry night atmosphere with fluid card animations
• Reversed cards with distinct upright/reversed meanings
• Save & share your reading as an elegant image
• Full Turkish + English language support
• Sound and haptic preferences

🔒 PRIVACY FIRST
No accounts, no ads, no tracking, no data collection. Everything stays on your device.

Mystic Tarot is for entertainment purposes only.
